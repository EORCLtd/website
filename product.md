# Decisions intelligence under operational and strategic uncertainty.

Our technology. Advanced optimisation and machine learning methods with explicit representations of uncertainty, operating at scale across energy carriers, geographic regions and decision timescales.

From data to decision:

1. **Data analytics:** historical data, scenarios and your assumptions.
2. **Scenario generation:** possible futures on operational and strategic time horizons.
3. **Modelling:** modular energy system model library.
4. **Algorithms:** algorithm tool box solving complex problems fast.
5. **Results analytics:** prices, pathways, valuations and risk.

Step 01 · Data Analytics

- Up-to-date European energy system database covering multiple energy carriers.
- Decades of high resolution time series data for renewable and energy demand
- Flexible and user friendly data management interface allowing you to input your own data and assumptions

Step 02 · Scenario generation

Proprietary scenario generation algorithms to help you capture uncertainty on both operational and strategic time horizons.
