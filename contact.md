# Talk to us about your system.

Email [info@eorc.uk](mailto:info@eorc.uk). EORC is based in Edinburgh, United Kingdom.

## See the platform on a problem like yours.

To request a demo, tell us about your system, decisions and difficulty in modelling uncertainty you are facing, we'll show you how the platform handles them.

Your email should include:

- Your organisation and role
- The system or portfolio, and the energy carriers involved
- The decision you're facing
- A preferred time for a call

[Email us to book a demo](mailto:info@eorc.uk?subject=Demo%20request%20%E2%80%94%20EORC%20platform)

## From your email to a walkthrough.

### Send the email

It opens with the fields above already in place. A line on each is enough.

### We reply with a slot

We read your details and suggest a time for a call.

### A demo on your problem

We walk through the platform on a case shaped like yours.

## Partnerships, research or anything else.

General enquiries, partnerships, or questions about our work. Your email should include:

- Your organisation and role
- Your question

## Your question goes to the right person.

### Send the email

Tell us what it is about, in as much detail as you like.

### We pass it on

It goes to the person on the team best placed to answer.

### We reply

You hear back directly from them.
