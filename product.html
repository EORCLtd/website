02 · Uncertainty

Uncertainty, modelled explicitly

Unlike deterministic planning tools, our models represent uncertainty directly within the optimisation problem — demand evolution, renewable generation, fuel prices, policy assumptions and technology costs.

Decisions are evaluated for robustness — not optimality under a single assumed future.

03 · Algorithms

Scalable optimisation and AI algorithms

Formulations and solution techniques chosen to balance computational tractability with modelling fidelity — enabling analysis at high spatial, temporal and technological resolution.
