# Rigorous methods for uncertain futures.

About EORC. Edinburgh Operational Research Corporation is a technology company specialising in optimisation and decision support under uncertainty for energy systems.

## Who we are

EORC is a technology company specialising in making decision intelligence under uncertainty for energy systems accessible via optimisation and machine learning

## Our focus

Operational research, applied mathematics and energy systems analysis — with explicit representations of uncertainty: demand evolution, technology costs, policy and market dynamics.

## How we work

We prioritise making advanced modelling and tools accessible and interpretable to asset owners, government agencies, system operators, market analysts from all backgrounds.
