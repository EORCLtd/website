Understanding uncertainty
Defining uncertainty

Nobody knows tomorrow's prices, weather or demand.

Uncertainty is not knowing how things will turn out. A forecast gives one answer, but many futures are possible. We call each one a scenario.

  • energy demand
  • wind and solar output
  • fuel prices
  • government policy
Why it matters

A decision built on one forecast can fail.

It only works if that forecast comes true. Testing a decision against many scenarios shows whether it still holds when things turn out differently.
